Our Story

From humble kitchens to a Michelin-starred journey

Chef Alain Ducasse, Founder

Milestones

The Aurora Journey

1995

The Dream Begins

A young Alain Ducasse, working in his grandmother's kitchen in Tuscany, discovers his passion for creating dishes that bring people together.

2005

World Exploration

After a decade training in Michelin-starred kitchens across Europe and Asia, Chef Rossi develops his signature style blending Italian heritage with global techniques.

2015

Aurora is Born

In a historic building on Culinary Avenue, Aurora Bistro opens its doors. The name reflects the beauty of new beginnings and the light of inspiration.

2018

First Michelin Star

Aurora receives its first Michelin star, recognition of the team's dedication to excellence and their commitment to creating unforgettable dining experiences.

Today

The Journey Continues

Now a beloved culinary destination, Aurora continues to evolve while staying true to its founding vision: creating extraordinary experiences through food.

Looking Forward

Our Vision

As we look to the future, our commitment remains unchanged: to create moments of joy and connection through exceptional cuisine. We continue to push boundaries, explore new techniques, and nurture the next generation of culinary talent.

Every evening at Aurora is an opportunity to write a new chapter, to surprise and delight, and to remind our guests why the shared experience of a beautiful meal is one of life's greatest pleasures.
